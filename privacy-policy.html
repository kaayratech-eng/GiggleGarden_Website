GiggleGarden — Privacy Policy

GiggleGarden does not collect, store, or process personal data from any third party.

The tool interacts only with the operator's own connected social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) via each platform's official API, using credentials the operator controls directly, solely to publish video content the operator created.

No end-user data, analytics, or third-party information is collected, stored, or shared.
