Kaayra Tech — Privacy Policy

Kaayra Tech's publishing tools — used for brands including GiggleGarden and Chronicle & Chaos — do not collect, store, or process personal data from any third party.

These tools interact only with Kaayra Tech's own connected social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) for each brand, via each platform's official API, using credentials Kaayra Tech controls directly, solely to publish video content Kaayra Tech created.

No end-user data, analytics, or third-party information is collected, stored, or shared.
